COMPUTING / PROFILE COLLECTION 02

Games that changed
the experience.

Four very different games, each showing how programmers and designers found new ways to make computers feel like places to explore, stories to inhabit or puzzles to master.

THE LINE-UP

Four games, four ideas.

Chosen for their connection to British computing, distinctive design and lasting influence.

01 / GAME PROFILENEXT STAGE

Elite

An open-ended universe of trading, exploration and combat that made an extraordinary impression on 8-bit computers.

1984 · Space trading and combat
02 / GAME PROFILENEXT STAGE

Manic Miner

A sharply designed platform classic whose screens, hazards and loading sequence became part of Spectrum folklore.

1983 · Platform game
03 / GAME PROFILENEXT STAGE

The Hobbit

A literary adventure that combined a parser, graphics and a living-world approach to Tolkien's story.

1982 · Illustrated text adventure
04 / GAME PROFILENEXT STAGE

Lemmings

A deceptively simple rescue puzzle that turned tiny autonomous characters into a worldwide phenomenon.

1991 · Puzzle game
