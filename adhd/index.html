03 — PERSONAL JOURNEY

ADHD & Me.

An honest, evolving account of understanding how my mind works, what I discover and what helps in everyday life.

UNDERSTANDING, NOT DEFINING

A journey with more questions than shortcuts.

This space is intended for personal experience, reflection and useful resources. It isn't medical advice, and individual experiences with ADHD and treatment vary.

Over time, entries may explore diagnosis, titration, concentration, motivation, organisation, strategies and the things that turn out to be useful.

A note on privacy: this starter page is public. Keep personal or medical details out of public posts until a private writing and publishing workflow is in place.
