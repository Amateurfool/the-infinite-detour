05 / EVERYDAY FAITH

Living My
Faith.

Faith is more than something to think about. It matters in the way we approach life and the way we treat the people around us.

BELIEF / ACTION / EVERYDAY LIFE
THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

BEYOND WORDS

Faith should have
a place in everyday life.

For me, faith isn't simply something to think about on a Sunday, or something confined to a church, a building or a particular set of religious practices.

It should have a place in everyday life.

It should influence how we treat people, how we respond to difficult situations, how we show compassion, and how willing we are to accept others.

I don't expect everyone to share my beliefs. I don't believe that they need to.

We all have our own lives, experiences and paths to follow. Respecting that is important.

THE EVERYDAY EXPRESSION

Small things can carry meaning.

Not rules for other people. Reminders of the kind of person I want to be.

01

How we treat people

Meeting people with kindness and dignity, even when we see the world differently.

02

How we respond

Making space for patience, compassion and thought, especially when a situation is difficult.

03

How we accept others

Respecting that each person has their own choices, experiences and path through life.

04

How we keep learning

Staying open to reflection, recognising our own limitations and being willing to grow.

THE THOUGHT THAT GUIDES THIS SECTION

All are welcome.
All are accepted.
All are loved.

That is the spirit in which I want to share this journey.
