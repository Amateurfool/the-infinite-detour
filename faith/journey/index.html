with Jesus.

An unexpected invitation in 1994 became the beginning of a journey that has stayed with me ever since.

A STORY STILL UNFOLDING
AN UNEXPECTED BEGINNING

AN UNEXPECTED BEGINNING

In the early 1990s, I had some issues in my life, which I handled badly. I caused a fair amount of pain and hurt to people I cared about. I was in a very bad place, and things were getting progressively worse.

Then, in early 1994, something rather unexpected happened.

Through a strange set of circumstances, I received three separate invitations in one evening to attend a church service at a place called Vineyard.

Apparently, things were quite relaxed there. They even had a proper bar, and smoking was still allowed at the time.

Ordinarily, I would probably have dismissed the invitations without giving them a second thought. But the third came from an old work colleague who had undergone something of a transformation himself. That caught my attention and piqued my curiosity.

Eventually, Sunday arrived.

I'd spent much of the afternoon indulging, so I was hardly in a balanced state of mind when I turned up. I probably wasn't looking particularly presentable either. At that point, baths were something of a rare occurrence, although I had at least managed to put on some clean clothes. Every little helps!

I was greeted warmly by a rather posh-sounding bloke called Chris, who guided me inside. There were a couple of familiar faces, which helped.

But then I spotted something that really caught my attention.

One of the largest drum kits I'd ever seen.

This thing was full-on professional rock and roll. Double bass drums, toms everywhere, cymbals all over the place. It was EPIC!

There were guitars, keyboards and enormous speakers too.

I remember wondering whether I'd actually walked into a church or a concert.

I'd expected a piano, perhaps some hymn books and the sort of thing I'd previously experienced a church service to be.

What I found was something else entirely.

The service began, the band started playing, and although I didn't recognise any of the songs, it all sounded fantastic. There wasn't even a break between songs. They just kept playing, creating an atmosphere that was powerful and unlike anything I'd expected.

Then someone got up to speak.

I would later discover his name was Steve.

I couldn't tell you now exactly what he spoke about. But for nearly an hour, I had the extraordinary feeling that he was speaking directly to me. It was as though he could see right through me, looking deep into my soul and giving me a glimpse of something greater.

Something had definitely happened.

Towards the end of the service, someone offered to pray for me. And it was during that prayer that I experienced something I had never known before.

Love.
Pure, undiluted Love.

At the same time, I saw myself hiding in a box to keep the world out. The lid of the box opened, and a voice said:

“You can come out now, it's perfectly safe.”

I knew without a doubt who the words came from.

It wasn't a case of discovering someone new. It was more like recognising someone who had always been there, just waiting for me to look.

I don't pretend to understand everything that happened that evening, or to have all the answers about why I was there. But the feeling of that Love, and those words, have stayed with me.

And so began a journey that has taken me to some interesting places.
