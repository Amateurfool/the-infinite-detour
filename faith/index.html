A PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE

Faith is personal.
So is the journey.

My faith is rooted in a personal relationship and spiritual connection with Jesus. For me, it is a source of guidance and acceptance, but it is also something to think about, question and understand in my own way.

I don't want to impose my beliefs or philosophy on anyone else. We should each be free to live our lives in the way we individually choose. This is simply a place to share my perspective, not to tell anyone else what theirs should be.
