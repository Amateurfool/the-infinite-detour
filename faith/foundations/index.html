02 / WHAT MATTERS

The Foundations.

For me, faith starts with the things Jesus taught about how we live, how we treat people and how we make room for one another.

LOVE / COMPASSION / ACCEPTANCE
THE THINGS THAT MATTER

A PLACE TO BEGIN

Simple ideas.
Not always easy to live.

For me, faith isn't about having all the answers, following a set of rules without question, or believing that one person has all the right interpretations.

It starts with something much simpler: the foundations of what Jesus taught.

Love, compassion, forgiveness, acceptance and humility. The importance of how we treat one another. Recognising the value of every individual, and treating others with kindness and dignity.

These aren't complicated ideas, but they can be surprisingly difficult to live by.

I believe these foundations matter. They offer something meaningful to build a life around, regardless of where someone happens to be on their own spiritual journey.

THE VALUES

What sits at the heart.

Not a checklist for judging others. A set of principles to keep returning to.

01

Love

Love as the starting point: not reserved for people who think, behave or believe exactly as we do.

02

Compassion

Trying to understand what someone else may be carrying, and responding with care rather than judgement.

03

Forgiveness

Making room for the possibility of change, recognising our own failings and not defining people only by their past.

04

Acceptance

Allowing people to be who they are, without making belonging conditional on agreement.

05

Humility

Remembering that none of us has a complete understanding, and that there is always more to learn.

06

Dignity

Treating every person as someone whose life, experiences and choices deserve respect.

A THOUGHT TO CARRY

Belief means little
if it doesn't shape how we treat people.

This page will grow as I explore these foundations in more depth. For now, these are the principles I keep coming back to.
