CURIOSITY / REFLECTION / OPENNESS
NOT EVERY PATH NEEDS A MAP

ROOM TO THINK

Faith and questions
can belong together.

I don't think faith and questions are opposites.

In fact, I think the willingness to ask questions is an important part of any genuine journey of understanding.

There are things I believe, things I'm still trying to understand, and things I may never have a definitive answer to.

That doesn't trouble me.

This isn't a place where every question needs to end with a neat conclusion. It's a place to explore ideas, consider different perspectives and be honest about the things we don't know.

Sometimes the value is in asking the question in the first place.

QUESTIONS TO EXPLORE

Open doors, not final answers.

These are starting points. They are invitations to think, not tests with a right answer.

01

How do we distinguish the teachings of Jesus from later interpretations?

What helps us understand the difference between a teaching, the way it is interpreted and the way it is put into practice?

02

Can faith be personal without becoming isolated?

What place do community, shared worship and individual reflection each have in a person's journey?

03

What does acceptance really look like?

How do we welcome people whose lives, choices or beliefs are different from our own?

04

Is it possible to question tradition while respecting those who value it?

How can honest disagreement make room for understanding rather than division?

05

What does it mean to live the values we say we believe in?

How do love, compassion and forgiveness show up in ordinary decisions and relationships?

NO RUSH TO CONCLUDE

Some questions are part
of the journey itself.

As new questions arise, this page will grow. Some may lead to reflections, others to further reading, and some may simply remain open.
