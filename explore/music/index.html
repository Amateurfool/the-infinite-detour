THE FIELD GUIDE

A listening guide: what makes up a sound?

The sound we hear is shaped by several interacting elements. Pitch gives a note its high or low quality; rhythm organises events in time; timbre gives an instrument its recognisable character; dynamics shape intensity; and arrangement determines how the parts fit together.

01

Timbre

The character or colour of a sound — why the same note differs between instruments.

02

Dynamics

Changes in loudness and intensity, from subtle variation to dramatic contrast.

03

Signal chain

The route an instrument's signal takes through effects, amplification and recording.
