THE FIELD GUIDE

Reading a place: a field guide

Start with the shape of the landscape, then look at how people have adapted to it. Routes, buildings, industries, food, local traditions and public spaces all offer clues. A little context can turn a quick visit into a much richer experience.

01

Before you go

Check access, opening arrangements, transport and seasonal conditions using current official information.

02

Look beyond the landmark

Explore the surrounding streets, landscape and local history to understand the wider setting.

03

Keep a sense of scale

Distances on a map do not always reflect travel time, terrain or ease of access.
