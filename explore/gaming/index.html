THE FIELD GUIDE

Understanding the anatomy of a game

A game is an arrangement of rules, goals, feedback and player choices. The most compelling experiences often emerge when these elements work together: the player understands enough to make meaningful decisions, but still has room to experiment and be surprised.

01

Core loop

The repeatable sequence of actions that forms the basic rhythm of play.

02

Progression

How a game changes what the player can do through skills, equipment, knowledge or access.

03

Emergent play

Unexpected situations created by the interaction of systems, rules and player decisions.
