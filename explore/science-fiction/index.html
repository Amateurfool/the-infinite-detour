THE FIELD GUIDE

A small field guide to speculative fiction

Science fiction asks what might be possible if technology, society or our understanding of the universe changed. Fantasy often introduces magic, myth or supernatural forces as part of its reality. The boundaries are porous, and some of the most interesting stories happily ignore them.

01

A useful distinction

Science fiction often builds from imagined possibilities; fantasy often builds from the impossible made real. Neither definition is a rule.

02

World-building checklist

Consider place, history, power, belief, everyday life and the limits that shape the world.

03

A question worth keeping

What does this imagined world allow its characters to do — and what does it make difficult?
