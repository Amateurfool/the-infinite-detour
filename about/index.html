A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A mind that rarely takes the direct route.

I'm someone with a fairly broad range of interests, an ongoing curiosity about how things work, and a habit of finding something interesting in places I wasn't necessarily looking.

Music, technology, computing, gaming, writing and learning all have a place here. Some interests are longstanding, others are relatively recent discoveries, and a few have a habit of becoming considerably more involved than originally intended.

01THE REASON FOR THE DETOUR

WHY THIS PLACE EXISTS

A place for all the different threads.

The Infinite Detour is where those interests come together. Not as a carefully organised collection of achievements, but as a record of exploration, experimentation, ideas and the occasional unexpected detour.

I've never been especially drawn to keeping everything in its own neat little box. One subject can lead to another; a passing question can turn into a project; something picked up out of curiosity can become a much bigger part of the picture.

This site is part notebook, part workshop, part collection of things worth following. Some projects will become substantial. Others may be no more than a thought that deserved writing down.

Not everything needs to become something.
A few beginnings.

02HOW THE MIND WANDERS
THE ROUTE IS RARELY A STRAIGHT LINE

FOLLOWING THE THREAD

One thing leads to another.

Sometimes an idea is interesting because of what it is. Sometimes it's interesting because of what it connects to.

A question about how something works can lead into its history, the people who developed it, the technology behind it, or an entirely different subject that happens to share the same underlying idea.

That tendency to follow the thread is probably one of the things that ties all these interests together. It can make for a winding route, but it rarely leaves a shortage of things to explore.

✳ALWAYS IN PROGRESS

STILL FIGURING THINGS OUT

There is always more to understand.

Learning doesn't stop when a subject becomes familiar. Interests change, new questions appear, and sometimes understanding something better means looking at yourself a little differently too.

That personal side of the journey has its own space here. It's part of the wider picture, but it deserves room to be explored in its own way.

03AN OPEN NOTEBOOK

WORK IN PROGRESS

Nothing here is necessarily finished.

The website will evolve as the interests and projects behind it evolve. Pages will change, ideas will develop, some experiments will work and others probably won't.

There may be finished pieces, ongoing projects, half-formed thoughts and things that turn out to be interesting for reasons I hadn't expected.

The point isn't to create an imaginary finished destination. It's to make a little room for the process — and to leave enough space for the next unexpected turn.
