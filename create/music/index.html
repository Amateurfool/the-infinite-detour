04 / STILL TAKING SHAPE

Works in progress.

A riff recorded on the fly, a melody that needs somewhere to go, a sound that might fit another project. These are not failed finished pieces; they are part of the process.

This space can hold rough demos, short clips, alternate versions and notes about what might happen next. The unfinished work belongs here too.

06 / NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

Behind the sound.

Ideas rarely arrive fully formed. There are experiments, changes of direction, happy accidents and moments when a small adjustment makes everything fall into place.

Process notes can capture how a piece develops, what was tried, what worked and what might be worth exploring next. Technical learning and equipment research can link out to Learning, leaving this page focused on the creative decisions.
