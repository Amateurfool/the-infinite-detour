02 / UNDER THE SURFACE

Pull the mechanics apart.

Sometimes the most interesting part of a game is the system underneath it. This is a place to explore how an idea works, test alternatives and follow the unexpected results.

  • Gameplay rules and mechanics
  • Controls, interfaces and interaction
  • Procedural ideas and generated worlds

04 / THE DEVELOPMENT LOG

Notes from the process.

Making something interactive is rarely a straight line. A mechanic might work differently than expected; a prototype might reveal a better idea; a promising feature might need to be stripped back.

Development notes will capture the decisions, dead ends, discoveries and small breakthroughs behind each project — the useful bits that don't always make it into the finished thing.
