02 / BEHIND THE FICTION

Worlds have lives of their own.

A convincing setting is more than a backdrop. It has a past, a geography, people with their own stories, and details that suggest a much bigger world beyond the page.

  • Places, maps and environments
  • Histories, cultures and factions
  • Characters, relationships and motivations
  • Rules, lore and the details that hold it together
