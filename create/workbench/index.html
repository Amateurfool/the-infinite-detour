03 / A NOTE ABOUT THE PROCESS

Room to change your mind.

Not every experiment needs to become a finished piece. Some ideas will be parked, some will be picked up again much later, and some will turn out to be useful in ways that weren't obvious at first. This page will grow around real work as it appears — there are no invented projects or filler entries to keep it looking busy.
