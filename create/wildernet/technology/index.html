THE PLATFORM / THE EXPERIENCE

A world entered
through immersion.

The Wildernet is a full-immersion neural MMORPG created by Horizon Interactive. Players do not simply look at a screen and direct an avatar: the premise is that they enter and experience a persistent virtual world.

The story establishes the neural, full-immersion nature of the game. It has not yet set out a complete technical specification for the hardware, connection process or safeguards, so those details remain open rather than being filled in with invented explanations.

01 / CREATOR

Horizon Interactive

The company behind the Wildernet and the technology that makes its full-immersion experience possible.

02 / INTERFACE

Neural immersion

The established premise is direct neural immersion into the game world. The precise interface and operating method have not yet been fully described.

03 / ENVIRONMENT

A persistent world

The Wildernet is presented as a large open world in which players can explore, trade, fight, build relationships and follow different paths.

REALITY / VIRTUALITY

Where does the player
begin — and the world?

The distinction between the player's life outside the game and their experience inside it is central to the premise. The manuscript has not yet established every practical detail of that relationship. This page will grow as those details become part of the story.
