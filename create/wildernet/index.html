01 / THE PREMISE

A game that feels like
another life.

The Wildernet is a full-immersion neural MMORPG created by Horizon Interactive — a vast, persistent world where players can choose their own paths and make their own stories.

It promises the freedom of an open universe: exploration, trade, combat, discovery and the chance to become almost anything. Some arrive with a plan. Others simply want to see what is out there. The world has room for both.

But the Wildernet is not just a backdrop for adventure. Its history runs deep, its mysteries are not all understood, and the line between what players know and what the universe contains is not always where they expect it to be.

03 / THE STORY

Every beginning
is a departure.

Welcome to Everything follows a group of new players entering a world that promises limitless possibility. They arrive with different expectations, different experience and very different ideas about what makes a good adventure.

What begins as a journey into a new game grows into something far larger: a story of exploration, unlikely teamwork, dangerous discoveries and the consequences of finding things that perhaps were never meant to be found.

A spoiler-light introduction. The deeper history, major revelations and later turns are being kept back for now.

05 / BEHIND THE CREATION

A world still
being discovered.

The Wildernet is an evolving creative project: part story, part world-building exercise, part exploration of what happens when a game world is treated as a place with its own history and momentum.

This page will grow alongside the project. Over time, it can become a home for character profiles, locations, maps, lore, development notes and selected extracts — with room to distinguish established canon from ideas still taking shape.
