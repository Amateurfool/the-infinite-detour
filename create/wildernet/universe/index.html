A WORLD OF INTERSECTIONS

Not just a setting.
A system of stories.

The Wildernet is presented as a full-immersion neural MMORPG created by Horizon Interactive. Its open-world promise brings together exploration, trade, combat, discovery and many possible player identities. The setting extends beyond a single city, crew or storyline.
