THE PLAYER EXPERIENCE / FIND YOUR WAY

There is more than
one way to live here.

The Wildernet is designed around choice. Its open-world promise gives players room to decide what kind of life they want to pursue, whether that means making a living, seeking danger, exploring the unknown or finding a crew to call their own.

01 / DISCOVERY

Explore

Travel beyond familiar places, encounter unfamiliar environments and discover what lies beyond the known map.

02 / COMMERCE

Trade

Make connections, exchange goods and pursue opportunity in a world where different players value different things.

03 / PURSUIT

Bounty hunting

Take on the role of a hunter, follow a target and navigate the risks that come with the work.

04 / CONTRACTS

Mercenary work

Put your skills to use on jobs where preparation, capability and the people beside you can matter.

05 / THE EDGE

Piracy

Choose a more dangerous path on the frontier, where freedom and risk often travel together.

06 / ORDER

Law enforcement

Take a role on the other side of the law and engage with the conflicts that shape life across the world.

07 / CONFLICT

Combat & survival

Face hostile encounters, manage danger and learn that choices can carry consequences.

08 / CONNECTION

Cooperation

Work with other players, form a crew and discover what can be achieved together that would be difficult alone.

No single path defines the Wildernet.

These are broad player paths established by the world-building so far, not a complete list of formal classes or guaranteed game mechanics. The details of how each activity is structured will be added when the manuscript establishes them.
