FIELD GUIDE / PLAYER SYSTEMS

Know the rules.
Respect the consequences.

This is a practical guide to the systems that have appeared in the story so far. It is deliberately limited to confirmed details; not every mechanic or progression rule has been defined yet.

01
PROGRESSION

Levels & experience

Characters have levels, and experience points contribute to progression. The manuscript establishes that XP can be lost as a consequence of death. A complete experience table or level-up formula has not yet been published here.

CONFIRMED
LEVELS · XP
02
CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT

Skills & professions

Characters can develop practical abilities and professional skills. Vehicle Operation Novice I and Heavy Equipment Operator are examples already encountered in the story. The full skill tree and training rules remain to be established.

EXAMPLES
VEHICLE OPERATION · HEAVY EQUIPMENT
03
RESOURCES

Inventory & equipment

Players carry equipment and consumable items, and inventory can be affected by death. The exact inventory limits, item categories and equipment rules have not yet been fully documented.

CONFIRMED
ITEMS · EQUIPMENT
04
RISK / CONSEQUENCE

Death & respawning

Death is temporary, but it has a cost. The established penalty is the loss of 10% of acquired XP and one random inventory item. The later respawn process takes 30 seconds.

Death is temporary. Its consequences are not.
CONFIRMED
−10% ACQUIRED XP
1 RANDOM INVENTORY ITEM
30 SECOND RESPAWN
05
TRANSPORT

Vehicles & ships

Vehicles and spacecraft are part of the world. The crew's damaged Covenant Pathfinder vessel, the Wanderer, is a significant example. Specific vehicle handling, ownership and upgrade systems will be documented as they become clear.

EXAMPLE
THE WANDERER
06
CAPABILITY

Practical abilities

Not every useful capability is necessarily a combat skill. The story has already shown the importance of operating and handling equipment. More professions and practical systems can be added as they appear.

DEVELOPING
MORE TO COME
